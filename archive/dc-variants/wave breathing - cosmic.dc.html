The Seat

Find a comfortable seat

Settle into a stable, easy posture — seated or lying down. Let the spine grow long, the shoulders soften, the hands come to rest. Nothing is forced here; this is simply alert, comfortable stillness.

Inhale Exhale
The Wave Breath

Breathe in slow waves

Inhale and exhale for equal durations — never quite filling, never quite emptying, and never holding. Keep the diaphragm free of tension until the rhythm becomes almost involuntary, like the tide drawing against the shore.

Body in Space

Between earth & cosmos

Sense yourself seated in vast space — rooted to the ground like a mountain, crowned by open sky. Below is earth; above is cosmos. You are the still axis that joins them.

Fill & Empty

Fill and empty the vessel

Let the whole body become one vessel. The in-breath fills and charges the skull and upper torso; the out-breath suffuses the pelvis and the legs — expanding upward into the cosmos, rooting downward into the earth.

Move the Prana

Move energy through the spine

Now add the current. Draw prana up from the base of the spine into the skull as you inhale; release it back down through the body as you exhale. Always visualize both the influx and the outflux.

Penetrate the Spine

Gather into the spinal channel

Finally, narrow the attention to the spinal channel itself. Let breath, energy and awareness move as a single current through the core of the spine. This is where it all comes together — the whole practice, breathing as one.
