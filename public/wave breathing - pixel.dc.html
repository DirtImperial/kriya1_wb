[ 01 ] THE SEAT

FIND A COMFORTABLE SEAT

Settle into a stable, easy posture — seated or lying down. Let the spine grow long, the shoulders soften, the hands rest. Nothing is forced here: just alert, comfortable stillness.

Inhale and exhale for equal durations — never quite filling, never quite emptying, never holding. Keep the diaphragm loose until the rhythm becomes almost involuntary, like tide against the shore.

[ 03 ] BODY IN SPACE

BETWEEN EARTH & COSMOS

Sense yourself seated in vast space — rooted to the ground like a mountain, crowned by open sky. Below is earth; above is cosmos. You are the still axis that joins them.

[ 04 ] FILL & EMPTY

FILL AND EMPTY THE VESSEL

The whole body becomes one vessel. The in-breath fills and “charges” the skull and upper torso; the out-breath suffuses the pelvis and legs — expanding into the cosmos, rooting into the earth.

[ 05 ] MOVE THE PRANA

MOVE ENERGY THROUGH THE SPINE

Now add the current. Draw prana up from the base of the spine into the skull as you inhale; release it back down through the body as you exhale. Always visualize the influx and the outflux.

[ 06 ] PENETRATE THE SPINE

GATHER INTO THE SPINAL CHANNEL

Finally, narrow attention to the spinal channel itself. Let breath, energy and awareness move as a single current through the core of the spine. This unifies everything — the whole practice, breathing as one.
